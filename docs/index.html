04  ·  How it works
Three stages
01

Private pricing

Our Horizen Vela app prices the card against private dealer comps inside an enclave, so the raw comps stay private. Tested locally today; live Vela deployment is in progress.

02

Price certificate

The appraisal is published as a price certificate on Robinhood Chain, with value band, confidence and risk tier.

03

Lending vault

The vault reads the certificate and lends stablecoins against the deposited slab, up to the tier's max LTV.
